About

People who know me would probably say I'm too curious, too detail-oriented, and a little bit of an overthinker. But honestly? That's what makes me good at what I do, whether that's solving a complex problem or thinking about how to improve something that already works fine. That curiosity was never about what's on the screen, it's about what's happening behind it, which is what pulled me toward backend systems and cloud infrastructure.

That interest took off at Intel, where I got to work hands-on with production cloud infrastructure and microservices at scale. It deepened while TAing Cloud Computing and Big Data at NYU, where explaining these concepts to students turned out to be one of the best ways to learn them myself. Somewhere along the way I realized I'm most in my element thinking through system design and architecture tradeoffs, how to make something scale, stay reliable, and still be simple to reason about. Then GenAI happened, and that same curiosity took over again, I wanted to know how it all actually worked, from architectures to LLMs to RAG pipelines. That's what pulled me toward applied AI and agentic systems, the intersection I'm most drawn to now, taking AI capabilities out of research papers and building them into real, production-grade software. That's the part I enjoy most.

Outside of work, I follow sports probably more than I should, if a team wears red, there's a good chance I'm already a fan, Manchester United, RCB, Ferrari, you name it. When I'm not on a badminton court, I'm usually playing the keyboard or out with a camera. New York makes that easy, there's always something worth shooting, and never a shortage of Broadway shows to catch.
